# CAFE DOS — Sample Client Answers

These are possible client answers written in a friendly first-person voice. They are a discussion draft, not actual client statements or approval. Questions 1–28 reflect our earlier planning choices where available; Questions 29–52 are suggested assumptions. The client should confirm or change every answer.

Mobile-app assumption: staff use a tablet-friendly app, with phone-friendly access where useful. Android/iPad support, printer compatibility, local equipment and offline device-to-device operation still need confirmation and testing. A mobile app alone does not guarantee that separate devices or printers work during an outage.

Prepared 3 November 2026.
